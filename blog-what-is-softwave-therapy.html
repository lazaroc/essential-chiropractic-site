SoftWave Therapy

What Is SoftWave Therapy? A Drug-Free Path to Healing

By Dr. Greg Davis, D.C. June 2, 2026 6 min read

If you've been told your only options for chronic pain are medication, injections, or surgery, there's a third path worth knowing about. SoftWave therapy is a regenerative technology that helps your body do what it already knows how to do — heal itself.

At Essential Chiropractic, we added SoftWave because it fills a real gap. Some injuries and conditions don't respond fully to adjustments alone, and not everyone wants to mask pain with pills. SoftWave gives us a way to address the source of the problem at the tissue level, without drugs and without downtime.

How SoftWave Therapy Works

SoftWave uses patented, unfocused acoustic waves that travel broadly and deeply into your tissue. Unlike older "focused" shockwave devices that concentrate energy on a single point, SoftWave spreads that energy across a wider area — which is both more comfortable and better for stimulating healing across a region.

When those acoustic waves reach injured tissue, they trigger a cascade of responses your body uses for repair:

  • They increase blood flow and the formation of new blood vessels in the area.
  • They activate and recruit your body's own resident stem cells to the site of injury.
  • They reduce inflammation that's been keeping the area "stuck" in a pain cycle.
  • They stimulate the production of growth factors that rebuild healthy tissue.

The key idea: SoftWave doesn't just quiet pain signals — it kick-starts an actual healing response, so the area can get stronger over time rather than simply feel numb for a few hours.

What Does SoftWave Treat?

SoftWave is most helpful for soft-tissue and joint conditions that have been slow to heal on their own. Patients in our office commonly use it for:

  • Chronic low back, neck, and shoulder pain
  • Knee pain and osteoarthritis
  • Plantar fasciitis and foot pain
  • Tennis elbow, rotator cuff issues, and other tendon injuries
  • Nerve-related pain and certain neuropathy symptoms

It also pairs naturally with chiropractic care. When we restore proper motion to a joint and improve the health of the surrounding tissue, the results tend to hold longer.

What to Expect at a Session

A typical SoftWave session takes only about 5 to 15 minutes. There's no anesthesia, no needles, and no recovery time — most people go right back to their day. You'll feel a gentle tapping sensation as the applicator moves over the treatment area. Many patients notice some relief after the first visit, though lasting change usually builds over a series of sessions, since you're rebuilding tissue, not just chasing symptoms.

Is It Right for You?

SoftWave is safe for most people, but it isn't for everyone — for example, it's generally avoided over areas with active cancer, blood-clotting disorders, or during pregnancy. The honest answer is that it depends on your specific situation, which is exactly why the first step is a conversation and an exam, not a guess.
