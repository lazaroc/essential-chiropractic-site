The Basics

What Is Pediatric Chiropractic Care?

Pediatric chiropractic care is the assessment and gentle adjustment of a child's spine and nervous system, using techniques modified for their size, weight, and stage of development. The goal is to support the function of the nervous system, which controls how the body grows, heals, moves, and regulates itself.

A child's spine and nervous system go through rapid changes during the first few years of life. Birth itself, along with early milestones like rolling, crawling, walking, and the inevitable falls that come with learning to move, can place stress on the spine. As children grow into toddlers, school-age kids, and teens, posture, backpacks, screen time, and sports add new loads on the body.

Pediatric chiropractic care looks for areas of tension or restriction in the spine and uses gentle adjustments to help restore normal motion and nerve communication.

Care that changes as your child grows

  • Newborns & infants — after birth and through early milestones
  • Toddlers — rolling, crawling, walking, and the falls along the way
  • School-age kids — posture, backpacks, and screen time
  • Teens — sports training, growth, and postural strain

Common Reasons Parents Visit

How Pediatric Chiropractic Care Can Support Your Child

Parents bring their children to our Lee's Summit office for many different reasons. Here are some of the most common.

Colic, Reflux & Digestive Discomfort

Infants who struggle with colic, reflux, gas, or general fussiness after feeding may be experiencing tension in the upper neck or middle back — areas closely connected to the nerves that supply the digestive system. Gentle adjustments aim to support normal nerve communication and ease that tension.

Ear Infections & Recurring Ear Issues

The eustachian tubes, which drain the middle ear, can become restricted when there is tension in the upper neck. Many parents bring their children in to support drainage and overall ear health, particularly when ear infections become a recurring pattern. Chiropractic care is not a substitute for medical evaluation of an active infection.

Breech Presentation & the Webster Technique

The Webster Technique is a specific chiropractic analysis and adjustment used during pregnancy. It focuses on the pelvis and surrounding ligaments and is often chosen by mothers whose babies are in a breech position. While the care is given to the mother, the goal is to support optimal positioning for the baby.

Constipation & Bowel Issues

The nerves that influence the digestive tract exit the spine at multiple levels. When children experience chronic constipation, gentle adjustments aim to support the nerves involved in normal bowel function alongside other lifestyle factors like hydration and diet.

Torticollis & Neck Tension

Some babies are born with or develop a strong preference for turning their head in one direction. This pattern, often called torticollis, can be addressed with very gentle cervical work. Our approach focuses on releasing tension and supporting full, comfortable range of motion.

Restlessness & Sleep Disturbances

When the nervous system is under tension, children often have a harder time settling, sleeping, or staying asleep. Parents often choose chiropractic care to support nervous system regulation as part of a broader healthy-sleep approach.

Posture, Backpacks & Growing Bodies

For older children and teens, postural strain from school backpacks, screen use, and sports training can show up as headaches, neck tension, or fatigue. We assess posture, recommend simple changes at home and school, and use age-appropriate adjustments to support healthy spinal development.

Chiropractic care is not a substitute for pediatric medical care. Please consult your child's pediatrician for medical concerns.
Our Technique

Gentle Adjustments Using Torque Release Technique

Torque Release Technique (TRT) is the primary method we use for our pediatric patients. It is an instrument-assisted technique that delivers a very low-force adjustment to specific points along the spine. There is no twisting and no popping.

Because the force is gentle and precise, TRT is well-suited for newborns, infants, toddlers, and children of every age. Many parents who were nervous about chiropractic care for their child are reassured the first time they see how light the contact actually is.

Common Questions

Pediatric Chiropractic Care: Common Questions

Is chiropractic care safe for babies and children?
Yes, when performed by a chiropractor trained in pediatric care. The pressure used during a pediatric adjustment is very light, with no twisting or popping. We tailor every adjustment to your child's age, size, and needs.
At what age can my baby see a chiropractor?
Babies can be checked within the first few days of life. Many parents bring their newborns in soon after birth, especially if labor was long or involved interventions.
How is a pediatric adjustment different from an adult adjustment?
Pediatric adjustments use far less force, no twisting, and no popping. We rely on gentle, instrument-assisted techniques modified for your child's age and size.
Can chiropractic help with colic and reflux?
Many parents bring their fussy infants to us specifically because of colic or reflux. We work on releasing tension in the upper spine and supporting nerve communication to the digestive system. Results vary by child.
Can chiropractic help with ear infections?
Parents often choose chiropractic care to support healthy drainage of the middle ear, particularly when ear infections are recurring. Chiropractic care is not a substitute for medical care of an active infection.
Can a chiropractor help support a breech baby?
The Webster Technique is a specific chiropractic analysis used during pregnancy to support pelvic balance and optimal fetal positioning. Care is given to the mother, with the goal of supporting natural positioning for the baby.
How often will my child need to come in?
That depends on what we find at the initial assessment and what you are hoping to address. We discuss a recommended schedule with you after the exam, and you are always in control of the care plan.
Does insurance cover pediatric chiropractic care?
Coverage varies by carrier and plan. Call us at (816) 895-1800 and we will help you confirm your coverage before your first visit.
What should I bring to my child's first appointment?
Bring your child's insurance information if applicable, a list of any current medications or concerns, and your child's pediatrician information. For infants, bring whatever helps keep them comfortable.
How will my child feel after their first adjustment?
Most children feel relaxed and unaffected, and many fall asleep during or after the visit. Some parents notice changes in sleep, mood, or digestion in the days that follow. Every child is different.
